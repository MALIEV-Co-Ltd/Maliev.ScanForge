## Registration-quality interpretation

Issue #3 requires independent accuracy and usable combined geometry but supplies no numerical RMS/overlap acceptance threshold. Native Global Fine Align's local `converged` flag tests an active sampled-pair mean below 0.001 mm; `stable` tests a change below 0.005 mm. Its integer coverage statistic counts records within 0.05 mm, including inactive records. These flags and sampled statistics do not replace full-geometry measurements. A false native convergence flag alone is neither a successful registration claim nor proof of geometric quality failure. Search tolerance 0.5 mm is a correspondence admission band, not an accuracy requirement.

Fine Align's `targetReached` is a different contract: full eligible-source evaluation must satisfy the explicitly configured overlap support and overlap RMS target. Plateau status is reported separately. The current quality assessment keeps complete-surface distributions, baseline-fixed overlap IDs, lost/gained support, directed scan-pair/union results, and reconstruction offset/components separate from numerical native parity. No pass threshold is invented to make an original-file replay green.
